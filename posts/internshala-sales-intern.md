# Internshala post: sales intern for Revamp

Task: [#6](https://github.com/topc-social/revamp-tasks/issues/6). Paste each block into the matching Internshala field. Kamal posts it himself.

## Rules this post follows

From [Internshala's terms](https://internshala.com/terms), read 2026-10-25:
- **Minimum stipend: ₹1,000/month for work from home** (₹2,000 in office). Unpaid internships aren't allowed for companies like TOPC, so commission alone won't pass. This post pays ₹1,000/month fixed plus 20% of each deal.
- **No free work disguised as an assessment.** The screening questions below are just questions, with no real task that we could use.
- **Applicants can't be asked to sell anything as part of shortlisting,** and roles built on selling to your own personal connections count as network marketing, which isn't allowed. So the post says to reach startups through public channels, not friends and family.
- **No fees of any kind** from applicants.
- **Pay on time, never less than advertised.**

Budget: one intern for 2 months = ₹2,000 fixed. Two interns = ₹4,000, inside the ₹5–7k Kamal set aside. Commission comes out of client money, so it never touches that budget.

**Profile:** Business Development (Sales)

**Type:** Work from home · Part time · 2 months · 2 openings

**Stipend:** Performance based. Fixed ₹1,000/month + incentives: 20% of every client's first payment (₹5,000 for each ₹25,000 deal)

**About the company**

TOPC is a small studio from Pune that builds products in the open. Revamp is one of them: we take an early startup's website and rebuild it as a landing page that actually moves, with scroll animations and a hero people remember. Most startup sites look the same. We think a founder's first impression shouldn't.

**About the internship**

You'll find early-stage startups whose websites could be better, and help them get a Revamp.

Day to day, you'll:
1. Find startups that just launched or just raised (Product Hunt, LinkedIn, startup news, X)
2. Look at their site and write down what we'd improve
3. Send them a short, personal message with a before/after example
4. Set up a call between the founder and Kamal, who handles the pitch and the build
5. Keep a simple sheet of who you reached, who replied and who said yes

What we sell: an animated landing page for ₹25,000, or an animated hero section for ₹10,000. You get 20% of the first payment for every client you bring in, paid within 7 days of the client paying us.

Please reach startups through public channels. We don't want you selling to your friends or family.

**Who can apply**
- Any year, any branch. 1st and 2nd year students are very welcome
- You like talking to people and you don't mind hearing no
- You can write a short, clear message in English
- You're curious about startups and how they get their first customers

**Skills:** English (written), Communication, Sales, LinkedIn

**Perks**
- Certificate and letter of recommendation
- Flexible hours, work from anywhere
- A line on your CV most students don't have: "brought in ₹X of real clients"
- You'll see how a studio wins and builds real client work, start to finish

**Screening questions**
1. Name a startup whose website you'd change. What would you change first, and why?
2. Tell us about a time you convinced someone of something, big or small.
3. How many hours a week can you give for the next 2 months?
