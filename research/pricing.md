## What this says

- In India, a plain landing page from a freelancer is ₹15–25k. Custom design moves it to ₹40–80k at agencies.
- Animated (GSAP) work sells at a premium. The one Indian GSAP bid found is ₹56k for 14 pages; Indian GSAP specialists on Contra price in dollars, around $1,500 for a week.
- Award-style studios price for funded companies, not early startups.
